Study Guide: “What If?” Analysis (Rardin Ch. 4)

Context: In the real world, requirements change. A good modeler doesn’t just solve the problem in the book; they adapt the structure when the client adds a new constraint or changes the objective.

How to use this guide:

  1. Read the Scenario.
  2. Cover the Solution and try to write the mathematical modification yourself.
  3. Check your answer.
