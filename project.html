Course project

Teams of one or two choose a case study, formulate and implement the model in Colab, analyze the results and write a memo for a decision maker. Details are in the project requirements.

Case studies

  1. Linear programming: hospital staffing and resource planning
  2. Integer programming: food bank distribution network design
  3. Network models: municipal water distribution
  4. Routing: grocery e-commerce delivery routes
  5. Stochastic LP: crop planning under uncertainty
  6. Large-scale LP/IP: airline crew scheduling
