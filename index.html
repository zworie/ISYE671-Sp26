Linear Optimization and Network Flows

Open course materials for ISYE 671. The course covers formulating and solving linear, integer, network, stochastic and large-scale optimization models, implemented in AMPL and Python on Google Colab, with generative AI used as a coding co-pilot under an explicit disclosure policy.

Instructor: Dr. Ziteng Wang, Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering

Getting started

  1. Read the notes. Each lecture note opens as a web page with rendered math; PDF or slide versions are linked where available.
  2. Run the labs in Colab. The “Colab” links open a notebook directly from this repository. No local installation is needed.
  3. Get a free AMPL license. Request an AMPL Community Edition license at ampl.com/ce (or a course license at ampl.com/courses) and paste your UUID where a notebook says YOUR-AMPL-LICENSE-UUID.

Textbook: R. L. Rardin, Optimization in Operations Research, 2nd ed., Pearson. Background: ISYE 370 or equivalent, plus basic coding.

Course project

Students pick one of six case studies and deliver a model, a Colab implementation, analysis and a managerial memo. See the project requirements.

  • Linear programming: hospital staffing and resource planning
  • Integer programming: food bank distribution network design
  • Network models: municipal water distribution
  • Routing: grocery e-commerce delivery routes
  • Stochastic LP: crop planning under uncertainty
  • Large-scale LP/IP: airline crew scheduling
