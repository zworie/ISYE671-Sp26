Linear Optimization and Network Flows

ISYE 671 · Graduate course · Northern Illinois University · Spring 2026
Instructor: Dr. Ziteng Wang, Industrial and Systems Engineering

This site shares the materials from the Spring 2026 offering of ISYE 671. Students learn to formulate linear, integer, network, stochastic and large-scale optimization models and to solve them with AMPL and Python in Google Colab. Generative AI is used as a coding assistant, with full disclosure required.

What's here

Materials15 lecture notes and slide decks, 9 Colab notebooks, in-class exercises
Homework5 assignments (solutions are not published)

SyllabusObjectives, grading, schedule and AI policy

Topics

  1. Linear optimization modeling
  2. Integer optimization
  3. Network flow models
  4. Advanced LP and IP models
  5. Optimization under uncertainty
  6. Large-scale methods
  7. Advanced topics and next steps

Running the labs

Each lab opens directly in Google Colab; nothing needs to be installed. The notebooks use AMPL, which needs a free license: request one at ampl.com/ce and paste the UUID where a notebook says YOUR-AMPL-LICENSE-UUID.

Textbook

R. L. Rardin, Optimization in Operations Research, 2nd ed., Pearson. Prerequisites: an undergraduate operations research course and basic programming.

All files can be downloaded as one zip or browsed on GitHub. Found an error? Open an issue.
