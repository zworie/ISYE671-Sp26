Running the labs

Labs open directly in Google Colab, so nothing needs installing. They use AMPL, which needs a free license: request one at ampl.com/ce and paste the UUID where a notebook says YOUR-AMPL-LICENSE-UUID.

Textbook

R. L. Rardin, Optimization in Operations Research, 2nd ed., Pearson. Prerequisites: an undergraduate operations research course and basic programming.
